# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

OLLITERATOR contributors are expected to keep technical discussion direct,
professional, and focused on the work.

Disagreement is welcome. Harassment is not.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- challenging assumptions with technical evidence;
- identifying unsafe behavior plainly;
- refusing changes that weaken fail-closed behavior;
- asking contributors to reproduce or prosecute hardware claims;
- correcting inaccurate documentation or test claims.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, or personal attacks;
- publishing another person's private information;
- deliberately misleading maintainers about test or prosecution results;
- knowingly representing unprosecuted hardware as supported.

## Safety discussions

OLLITERATOR performs intentionally destructive operations after authorization.
Safety findings should be treated seriously.

A technical objection to a destructive path, authorization predicate, hardware
profile, or prosecution claim is not a conduct violation merely because it is
direct.

Keep criticism aimed at the code, evidence, claim, or process.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions or
participation that violate this standard.

Security-sensitive findings should follow `SECURITY.md`.
